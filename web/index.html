bozkır

captured ground, placed by terrain

A few square metres of ground, captured as 3D Gaussian splats, laid over kilometres of terrain as Wang tiles. The contribution: which material goes where is computed from the terrain (drainage, topographic position, slope, altitude) instead of a coverage map painted by hand, as in Hybrid GSWT. Tiling, ordering and merging reimplement GSWT so this can be tested at scale. Runs in the browser on a laptop GPU.

Best on a computer: on a phone it runs, slowly, at reduced resolution.
